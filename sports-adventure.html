Tension, Slack & Stillness

Highliner & Slackliner

Highlining is like walking across a tightrope inside your own mind: if you fight the oscillation, it throws you off. You don't balance by tensing up; you balance by relaxing into the sway. Rigged 11,000 feet above the Suru Valley canyon floor, the void becomes a mirror. As Nietzsche wrote, "When you look long into an abyss, the abyss also looks into you." Out on that 1-inch line, stillness isn't the absence of movement: it is complete surrender to the tension.
