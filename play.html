Highliner & Slackliner

Highlining is 90% mental and 10% balance. Walking on a 1-inch strap across an 11,000 ft valley in Ladakh taught me one big rule: when things get shaky, taking a deep breath works way better than freezing up. It is the ultimate test of staying calm under pressure.
