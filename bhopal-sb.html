01 / Mission

Skate · Create · Compete · Belong

Skateboarding in Central India was practically non-existent with zero dedicated infrastructure or community support. Bhopal SB started with a simple belief: if the scene does not exist in your hometown, you build it yourself.

From teaching first-time riders how to balance on their boards to hosting high-energy night jams and competing at national skateboarding championships, Bhopal SB turns empty asphalt and public parks into inclusive hubs for self-expression and grit.

02 / Pillars

What We Build & Run

Coaching & Safety
Youth Pathways & Clinics

Structured 90-minute coaching sessions focused on stance, board control, drop-ins, and safety gear. Accessible to young kids and beginners starting at zero.

Culture & Street Jams
Events & Competitions

Over 19 street jams, bowl contests, and fire coping night sessions uniting skaters from Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore, and across Central India.

Makerspace & Hardware
The Lab & Custom Builds

Designing custom maple decks, fabricating portable grind rails, DIY ledges, and providing shared loaner skateboards for kids who cannot afford their own.

Nationwide Scene
Ecosystem Collaborations

Partnered with grassroots leaders across India including Decathlon, Skate Supply India, GSC Sk8 Gwalior, Janwaar Castle, and Kovalam Skate Club.
