01 / Foundation & Vision

Built with the Scene, for the Scene

Skateboarding in India has traditionally thrived in coastal hubs and metropolitan centers. Central India lacked dedicated infrastructure, structured coaching, and accessible gear. Bhopal SB was created to change that reality: transforming empty concrete spaces into thriving hubs of creative movement, discipline, and community mentorship.

What started with a single skateboard and a few curious kids in public parks has evolved into a registered grassroots movement. Today, Bhopal SB runs structured coaching pathways, weekend skate jams, DIY park repairs, and regional events that bring together skaters from across Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and North India.
